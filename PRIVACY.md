# Privacy

bondline doesn't collect or send any personal data. There's no server, account, analytics or telemetry.

Figures are rendered locally (in Claude's sandbox on claude.ai, or on your own machine in Claude Code) and only saved where you ask.

The only network access is:

- installing matplotlib and RDKit from PyPI on first use, if they're missing
- optional lookups on PubChem or ChEMBL when Claude needs to confirm a compound's structure

Questions: https://github.com/montybwolfe/bondline/issues
